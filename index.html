About the Lore

A storyteller's archive, built for action.

Lycan Lore was born from a simple frustration: the most compelling conservation and livelihood stories from Northeast India were being told in silos — to activists who already believed, or in academic journals nobody read.

We pair each community-led narrative with a verified Project Proposal — so that the investors, CSR teams, and international donors who are looking for exactly this kind of work can find it, trust it, and fund it.

Stories belong to the communities who told them. Revenue flows back to the ground. Every proposal is co-designed, costed, and consented to by the people who will lead it.
